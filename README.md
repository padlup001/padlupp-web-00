# Project Title

`PADLUPP`

## Project Vision/Business Goals
Individuals pursuing personal or professional goals often struggle with maintaining motivation, finding like-minded peers, and connecting with mentors who can guide them towards success. Despite numerous platforms available for social networking and productivity, there is a lack of focused solutions that integrate ai-learning, gamification, mentorship, and accountability in a seamless and engaging way. 
This gap leaves goal-oriented individuals, such as students, self-learners, and professionals, without the necessary tools to sustain motivation,find like-minded people, and achieve their objectives.
